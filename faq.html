Frequently Asked Questions

First look

What is the COSTAR Spiral-Tuned Guitar?
A web app that draws a guitar fretboard and shows where the notes of a key and mode fall on it, in colors. With a MIDI guitar or keyboard connected, it also lights up the notes you play. The note color sequence is that of the COSTAR system and its chromatic lunar-solar calendar.

Who is it for?
Guitarists who play in all fourths (E–A–D–G–C–F or similar), or want to try it. It can also show standard tuning, and can be helpful for anyone learning scales and modes on the neck. This app is focussed on explorations of All Fourths tuning.

Do I need to tune my guitar in fourths to use it?
No. Choose Standard under Settings > Instrument > Tuning to see the board in regular guitar tuning.

What does it cost? Do I need an account?
It's free, with no account and no sign-in.

Do I need to install anything?
No. It runs in a web browser.

Which devices does it work on?
Any computer, tablet or phone with a modern browser shows the board. Playing along with a MIDI instrument needs Chrome, Edge or another Chrome-family browser on Mac, Windows or Android.

Does it make sound?
No. It shows notes; it doesn't play them.

Getting around

Where are the settings?
Click the gear button at the top right, or press H.

How do I see the keyboard shortcuts?
Press K. A bubble listing every shortcut appears at the bottom of the screen; K again hides it.

How do I change the key or mode quickly?
Use the arrow keys: ← → for the key, ↑ ↓ for the mode.

Can I clear the clutter off the screen?
Yes. I hides the key badge, MIDI status, fret numbers and open-string names. F goes full screen. D shows only the notes you play.

Will it remember my settings?
Yes, in the same browser on the same device. A different browser, device or private window starts with the defaults.

How do I go back to the starting setup?
There's no reset button. Clearing this site's stored data in your browser's settings restores the defaults, and also removes your song presets.

Can I send someone the board exactly as I have it set?
Yes, by adding options to the web address, such as ?key=7&mode=dorian. The User Guide lists them all.

Can I use it without a keyboard, on a tablet?
Yes. Everything the shortcuts do is also in Settings.

Reading the board

What do the colors mean?
In a diatonic mode, each color marks a scale degree of the mode's relative major: 1 is half white and half black, 2 green, 3 yellow, 4 orange, 5 red, 6 blue and 7 purple. The same note keeps the same color whichever of the seven modes you pick from that major scale.

Why is the root of A Aeolian blue, not white/black?
A Aeolian uses the notes of C major, and A is the 6th degree of C major, so it's blue. The badge at the top left always names the root's color, and the note under the mode buttons in Settings names the relative major.

What are the black dots?
The luma sequence: the five notes the diatonic scale leaves out. Turn them on with L. In a pentatonic mode these five notes are the scale.

What do the Greek letters mean?
Each black dot's letter marks its distance above the relative major's 1: α = ♭2, β = ♭3, γ = ♭5, δ = ♭6, ε = ♭7. Show them (or the note name, or the scale degree) with G.

Why can't I turn on the colored dots in a pentatonic mode?
In a pentatonic mode the scale is the five black dots, so the app hides the colored ones to keep the scale clear. They come back when you return to a diatonic mode.

Can I show note names or scale degrees on the dots?
Yes. N cycles the colored dots' labels and G the black dots' labels.

What is the Twelve strings view?
In all fourths every string is the same distance from the next, so a scale shape repeats exactly as it moves across the strings. The Twelve strings view (V) continues that tuning to twelve rows a fourth apart, with your six real strings in the middle, so you can ponder an extended view of the shapes, even if guitar notes played will only display on the middle 6 strings.

What is a scale pattern?
One playable position of the scale, three notes per string for diatonic modes and two for pentatonic. Turn it on with P and slide it along the neck with [ and ].

Which way up is the neck drawn?
By default the high string is on top, as in guitar tab. O flips it to put the low string on top.

Guitars, keyboards and MIDI

What can I connect?
Any MIDI instrument your computer recognizes: a guitar-to-MIDI converter such as the Roland GR-20, a MIDI guitar, a keyboard, or recording software.

How do I connect it?
Plug it in, open the app in Chrome or Edge, and click Allow when the browser asks about MIDI devices. The MIDI status at the top right names the inputs found.

Why doesn't MIDI work on my iPhone, iPad or in Safari?
Those browsers don't support Web MIDI. The board and click-to-play still work there.

My guitar's notes light up on the wrong string. What do I change?
In Settings > MIDI > String mapping, try channel 1 = low string instead of channel 1 = high string, or the other way round. Your converter needs to send one channel per string (often called Mono mode).

I'm using a keyboard. Which mapping should I use?
Pitch only. Each key you press lights every position on the neck where that note can be played.

Notes show up an octave or a few steps off. Can I fix that?
Yes. Set Transpose incoming notes in Settings > MIDI, up to 24 half steps either way. Check your converter's own transpose setting first.

Can I try it without any MIDI gear?
Yes. Click and hold any position on the board to light it.

Can a footswitch or my recording software change songs?
Yes, with Song presets. Each preset holds a key and mode, and a MIDI Program Change loads the matching preset. Set Listen for Program Change on to the channel your controller sends on.

Troubleshooting

The MIDI status says MIDI is blocked.
Click the icon at the left of Chrome's address bar, allow MIDI devices, then reload the page.

My device isn't listed.
Check it's plugged in and switched on, then click Connect MIDI in Settings > MIDI. If you connected it while the browser was open and it still doesn't appear, quit the browser completely and reopen it.

I play but nothing lights up.
Look at Last note received in Settings > MIDI. If it stays at "—", no notes are arriving: check the Input setting and your device's MIDI output. If notes arrive but light in the wrong place, check the String mapping and Transpose settings.

A note stays lit after I stop playing.
Press Esc to clear stuck notes.

A shortcut key does nothing.
Shortcuts pause while a number box or menu in Settings is selected. Click the fretboard and try again. A notice at the top of the screen says why when a shortcut can't act, for example the chroma keys in a pentatonic mode.

Program Change doesn't load a preset.
Check that a preset exists with that number, that Listen for Program Change on matches the sending channel (or is set to Any), and that your software isn't counting from 0 while the app counts from 1.

The page looks out of date after an update.
Reload it with Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows).
